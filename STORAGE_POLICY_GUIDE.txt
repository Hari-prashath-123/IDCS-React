STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE: Adding Storage Policies for od-proofs

=============================================================================
POLICY 1: Allow Students to Upload (INSERT)
=============================================================================

1. Click "New policy" under "od-proofs" bucket
2. Choose "For full customization" or "Custom policy"
3. Fill in:

   Policy name: Students can upload own proofs
   
   Policy definition:
   - Check: INSERT
   
   Target roles: authenticated
   
   USING expression (leave blank for INSERT)
   
   WITH CHECK expression:
   (bucket_id = 'od-proofs' AND (storage.foldername(name))[1] = auth.uid()::text)

4. Click "Review" then "Save policy"

=============================================================================
POLICY 2: Allow Anyone to View (SELECT)
=============================================================================

1. Click "New policy" again
2. Choose "Custom policy"
3. Fill in:

   Policy name: Anyone can view proofs
   
   Policy definition:
   - Check: SELECT
   
   Target roles: public
   
   USING expression:
   (bucket_id = 'od-proofs')
   
   WITH CHECK expression (leave blank for SELECT)

4. Click "Review" then "Save policy"

=============================================================================
POLICY 3: Allow Students to Update (UPDATE)
=============================================================================

1. Click "New policy" again
2. Choose "Custom policy"
3. Fill in:

   Policy name: Students can update own proofs
   
   Policy definition:
   - Check: UPDATE
   
   Target roles: authenticated
   
   USING expression:
   (bucket_id = 'od-proofs' AND (storage.foldername(name))[1] = auth.uid()::text)
   
   WITH CHECK expression:
   (bucket_id = 'od-proofs' AND (storage.foldername(name))[1] = auth.uid()::text)

4. Click "Review" then "Save policy"

=============================================================================
POLICY 4: Allow Students to Delete (DELETE)
=============================================================================

1. Click "New policy" again
2. Choose "Custom policy"
3. Fill in:

   Policy name: Students can delete own proofs
   
   Policy definition:
   - Check: DELETE
   
   Target roles: authenticated
   
   USING expression:
   (bucket_id = 'od-proofs' AND (storage.foldername(name))[1] = auth.uid()::text)
   
   WITH CHECK expression (leave blank for DELETE)

4. Click "Review" then "Save policy"

=============================================================================

ALTERNATIVE QUICK FIX (If above is too complex):
Just add ONE simple policy that allows everything:

Policy name: Allow all operations
Policy definition: Check ALL (SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE)
Target roles: authenticated
USING expression: (bucket_id = 'od-proofs')
WITH CHECK expression: (bucket_id = 'od-proofs')

This is less secure but will work for testing.
